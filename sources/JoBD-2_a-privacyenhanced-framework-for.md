- **Abstract:** The exponential growth of Big Data in healthcare, particularly in AI-driven medical diagnostics, has raised critical concerns about data privacy in medical image classification. With over 30% of healthcare organizations worldwide experiencing data breaches in the past year, the demand for secure, privacy-preserving solutions is more urgent than ever. This study explores a federated learning approach combined with transfer learning to enhance privacy in medical image classification using ResNet and VGG16 architectures. Pre-trained on ImageNet and fine tuned on three specialized medical datasets TB chest X-rays, brain tumor MRI scans, and diabetic retinopathy images these models were deployed in a simulated multi-center healthcare environment.
